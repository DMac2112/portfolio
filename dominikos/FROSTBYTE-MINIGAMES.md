# Frostbyte minigames — Club Penguin shortlist for review

Researched 72 Club Penguin games (2005–2017, incl. party/event, app and console spin-offs). Below: the ones that
fit Chillmere, re-themed as **original** Frostbyte games. Tick the ones to build.

⚠️ **IP rule for all of these:** game *mechanics* are free to use; Club Penguin's names, characters (puffles,
Rockhopper, etc.), art, music, sounds and distinctive presentation (e.g. Card-Jitsu's belt/ninja card look) are
not. Every pick gets a new name, our own art, our own characters, and a story reason to exist.

Effort, in this codebase (Snowdrift Toss is the template scaffold): **S** ≤1 day · **M** 2–4 days · **L** 1–2 weeks.

## Skip (and why)

- **Card-Jitsu** (all 4) — its presentation is distinctive CP trade dress; would need a full original card game.
- **Catchin' Waves, Hydro Hopper** — no surf/boat-towing space in Chillmere's art.
- **DJ3K, SoundStudio** — music toys, low replay; Bell Rhythm covers music.
- **System Defender, Spy Drills, Field-Ops, PSA/EPF missions** — spy-agency story doesn't fit a cosy village.
- **Party/event and licensed games** (Star Wars, Medieval, Super Hero, Fair games) — one-off event content.
- **Multiplayer-only** (ice hockey, shootouts, pizza-eating contest) — Frostbyte has no online players.
- Already covered: snowball fights (Snowdrift Toss), scavenger hunts (curios), the daily spin (daily login).
